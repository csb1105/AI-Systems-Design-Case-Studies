## Problem Statement

Federal agencies maintain vast collections of policies, procedures, regulations, technical manuals, and operational guidance across multiple repositories. Employees often spend significant time searching for authoritative information, resulting in reduced productivity, inconsistent decision-making, and increased risk of relying on outdated or incomplete documentation.

Traditional keyword-based search systems frequently return large volumes of results without sufficient context, requiring users to manually review documents to locate relevant information. As information volumes continue to grow, agencies need a solution that enables accurate, secure, and traceable access to organizational knowledge through natural language interaction while ensuring responses remain grounded in approved source documents.
