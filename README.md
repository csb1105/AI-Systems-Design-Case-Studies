# AI-Systems-Design-Case-Studies
A collection of real-world and conceptual AI systems design case studies exploring how artificial intelligence solutions are architected, developed, deployed, and governed in production environments.

This repository examines the technical, operational, and strategic decisions involved in building scalable AI systems across machine learning, generative AI, computer vision, natural language processing, MLOps, and enterprise AI applications.

## Purpose
The goal of this repository is to bridge the gap between AI theory and real-world implementation by documenting:

- Business and mission requirements
- System architecture decisions
- Data engineering considerations
- Model selection and evaluation
- Infrastructure and deployment strategies
- Security and governance requirements
- Scalability and operational trade-offs
- Lessons learned and best practices

## Topics Covered
- AI Architecture and Solution Design
- Machine Learning Systems
- Generative AI and Large Language Models (LLMs)
- Computer Vision Applications
- Natural Language Processing (NLP)
- MLOps and Model Lifecycle Management
- Responsible AI and AI Governance
- Cloud and Enterprise AI Deployments
- AI for Government and Public Sector
- Performance, Reliability, and Security Engineering
