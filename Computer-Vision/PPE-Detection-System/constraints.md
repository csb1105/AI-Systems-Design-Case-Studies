# Constraints

## Technical Constraints
- Detection accuracy depends on camera placement, image quality, lighting, distance, and angle.
- The model must distinguish between personnel, PPE items, and visually similar objects.
- Real-time detection may be limited by network bandwidth, compute resources, and camera stream quality.
- The system must support confidence thresholds to reduce false positives and false negatives.
- Edge deployment may require optimized models with lower compute requirements.
- Detection performance may degrade when PPE designs, uniforms, or site conditions change.

## Operational Constraints
- Work environments may include occlusion, motion blur, poor lighting, weather exposure, and crowded scenes.
- PPE requirements may vary by facility, work zone, role, task, and hazard level.
- Alerts must be actionable without overwhelming safety teams.
- Human review may be required before using detections for enforcement decisions.
- Cameras may not cover all hazardous areas or worker movements.

## Governance Constraints
- Video and image monitoring must align with workplace privacy policies.
- Access to footage and detection records must be restricted.
- Detection logs must be auditable and governed by retention policies.
- Automated alerts should support safety intervention, not unsupported disciplinary action.
